AUTOMATION
If emergency stop is on, then no reply is sent.
If the person is operational or blocked, then the message is skipped.
If the person is unknown, then the message is held. No draft is guessed.
If you selected that contact for 1, 7, 15, or 30 days, then a reply can queue until that time ends. Price, payment, discount, and legal questions still wait for approval.
If mode is Approval, then you approve the draft first.

No scheduled task is saved. Price, time, and OTP are not guessed.
